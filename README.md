<h1>***📊 Diversity & Inclusion HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)***<h1>

<h2>Executive Summary</h2>
This project delivers a comprehensive Diversity & Inclusion (D&I) dashboard built in Power BI, with a sharp focus on gender balance across the entire employee lifecycle.


The primary objective was to transform raw HR data into clear visualizations, enabling stakeholders to track key performance indicators (KPIs) related to hiring, promotions, turnover, and performance ratings. The analysis explicitly highlights the critical challenge of slow progress in achieving gender balance at the executive management level, providing data-driven insights to inform and accelerate strategic D&I interventions.


<h2>Business Problem</h2>

The organization seeks to understand and mitigate systemic barriers preventing gender parity, particularly at senior leadership tiers. The dashboard was designed to answer crucial business questions:

1.How are hiring rates distributed by gender across different job levels?

2.Are promotions equitable, or is there a pipeline bottleneck at mid-to-senior levels?

3.Do turnover rates and performance ratings show a gender disparity, especially among high-performing employees?

4.What are the root causes for the low female representation (12.5%) at the Executive level?


<h2>Methodology</h2>
This project utilized standard Business Intelligence (BI) techniques within Power BI:

Data Ingestion: Raw HR dataset (including employee demographics, performance, and exit data) was imported.

Data Modeling and Transformation (DAX):

Created calculated measures using DAX to derive the six core KPIs (Hiring % Women, Promotion % Women, Turnover % Leavers, Executive Gender Balance, etc.).

Established data relationships and ensured the model supported complex slicing by Department, Job Level, Age group, and Region group.

Visualization: Designed an interactive, two-page dashboard focusing on data clarity and visual impact (as shown in the project images). Key visualizations include:

Stacked Bar Charts: For comparing hiring and performance ratings by gender.

Donut Charts (KPI 5): To clearly illustrate the severe gender gap at the Executive level.

Trend Lines/Bar Charts (KPI 2 & 3): To identify where the pipeline breaks down across different job levels.

<h2>Key Findings (Insights)</h2>

Hiring Funnel Leakage (KPI 1): While women make up 53% of hires at the Junior Officer level, their representation drops drastically to 13-14% at the Director and Executive hiring tiers, indicating a significant external or internal sourcing issue.

Promotion Bottleneck (KPI 2): The promotion rate for women drops sharply after the Manager level, showing a clear failure point in the internal pipeline to senior leadership roles.

High-Performer Turnover (KPI 3): The data suggests a concerning pattern: the average performance rating of female leavers (11% turnover) is higher than that of male leavers (9% turnover) at several senior job levels. The organization is losing its top female talent disproportionately.

Executive Gender Gap (KPI 5): The result of these combined issues is a stagnant Executive Gender Balance of 87.5% Male vs. 12.5% Female.

<h2>Business Recommendations</h2>

Implement Targeted Sponsorship: Launch a mandated sponsorship program for high-performing women currently in Manager and Senior Officer roles to actively prepare and accelerate their path to the Director level.

Investigate High-Performer Exits: Conduct an in-depth root cause analysis on the exit data for high-performing female leavers (those with a rating of 3 or 4). This should focus on understanding factors related to work-life balance, lack of career progression clarity, or culture.

Set Accountability for Senior Hiring: Introduce a metric in the dashboard to track and hold hiring managers accountable for D&I goals when recruiting for roles at the Director level and above.
